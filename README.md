A single sentence (176 characters) that explains the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from
things they already know: libraries, post offices, roads, letters, books.

> Imagine every library and post office in the world joined by roads so
> swift that your letter reaches any stranger, and the books of distant
> shelves open before you, in a blink.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
